Support Our School

Your generosity opens doors: providing braille materials, assistive technology and safe, accessible facilities for every learner.

Why It Matters

Disability is not inability

Our learners have extraordinary potential. With the right tools and support, there is no limit to what they can achieve.

Over the years, generous partners have helped provide a computer learning centre, learning materials, food, sports equipment and more. Yet needs remain: from braille papers and textbooks to safe, erosion-resistant pathways across campus. Every gift, large or small, makes a real difference.

Ways to Help

How you can make a difference

Give financially

Donations help provide braille materials, assistive technology, nutritious meals and facility improvements.

Donate materials

Braille paper, slates and styluses, white canes, textbooks and learning aids are always needed.

Partner & volunteer

Organisations and individuals can support skills training, sponsorships, facility projects and more.

Our Current Needs

What your support provides

Braille materials: papers, textbooks, slates and styluses for reading and writing.
Assistive technology: computers and tools that open up digital learning.
Safe facilities: accessible walkways and repairs that help learners move freely.
Nutritious meals: supporting health, concentration and growth.
Sports & recreation: equipment for play, teamwork and confidence.
