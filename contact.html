Find Us

Visiting Wa

The school is located in Wa, the capital of the Upper West Region of Ghana.

By road

Wa is connected by road to Tamale to the east and Burkina Faso to the north. Local transport (including the tricycles known as "yellow-yellows") can take you to the school on Masse Road.

Before you visit

We recommend calling ahead so we can welcome you properly and make sure the right people are available to meet you.

Accessibility

As a school for blind learners, our campus is designed with accessibility in mind. Let us know in advance if you need any assistance during your visit.
