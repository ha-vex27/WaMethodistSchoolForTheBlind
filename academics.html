Academics

A complete learning journey: from braille literacy and the national curriculum to rehabilitation and practical vocational skills.

Our Programmes

Learning for every stage and ability

We combine academic study, rehabilitation and vocational skills so that each learner can progress along the path that suits them best.

Basic School (KG – JHS)

Our learners study the full Ghana Education Service syllabus from kindergarten through junior high school, delivered in braille and large print. This prepares them for secondary education and beyond.

Rehabilitation Class

For learners who lose their sight after beginning their education, our rehabilitation class teaches braille reading and writing, helping them continue learning with confidence and independence.

Vocational Training

For learners who do not continue to senior high school, practical training builds real livelihoods, including soap making, weaving camp beds and chairs, and door mats, alongside crafts developed with our partners.

ICT & Assistive Technology

Our computer learning centre, established with the Force Foundation, UNESCO and the Ghana Society for the Blind, helps learners use technology to read, write, research and connect with the wider world.

Learning in Braille

Reading and writing without limits

Braille is the foundation of literacy here: a gateway to the same knowledge and opportunities available to every other child.

Our learners read and write in braille, use tactile materials and develop the orientation and mobility skills that make independent learning possible. Access to quality braille materials (papers, textbooks, slates and styluses) remains vital, and something our supporters help provide.

  • ✓Braille literacy across all subjects.
  • ✓Orientation & mobility for confident, safe movement.
  • ✓Assistive technology to open up digital learning.

Our Record

A tradition of achievement

Academic excellence

In past years the school achieved a 100% pass rate in the Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE) and has been recognised among the best in the Wa Municipality.

Competitive spirit

Our learners have won medals in inter-school competitions, showing talent and determination well beyond the classroom.

Pathways onward

Graduates move on to senior high school, further education and vocational careers, proof that ability always shines through.
