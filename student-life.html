Home · Student Life

Student Life

Beyond the classroom, our learners sing, create, compete and lead, building confidence and independence every day.

A Vibrant Community

Confidence in full voice

Our students are performers, creators and leaders. Music and the arts are a joyful part of life at Wa Methodist, and our learners have shared their remarkable talents with audiences far and wide.

Videos of our students singing have inspired people across Ghana and beyond, reminding everyone that talent and determination know no limits. These moments build the confidence that carries our learners through every challenge.

Life & Learning

Skills for independent living

Orientation & Mobility

Learners build the skills to move confidently and safely: at school, at home and in the community.

Daily Living Skills

Practical, everyday skills nurture self-reliance and dignity, from personal care to managing tasks independently.

Music & Culture

Our cultural troupe and love of music bring learners together, building teamwork, pride and joy.

Sports & Play

Recreation and sport build health, teamwork and confidence, and our learners have proudly won medals in competition.

Technology & ICT

Our computer learning centre helps learners explore digital skills, from reading and writing to research.

Friendship & Belonging

A caring residential community where every learner belongs, is supported and is encouraged to lead.

Creative Futures

Learning that creates opportunity

Through partnerships like the Nubuke Foundation, our learners explore design, crafts and entrepreneurial skills, turning creativity into confidence and, one day, into livelihoods.

These experiences show our students that their abilities can open doors: to work, to enterprise and to a future they shape themselves.
