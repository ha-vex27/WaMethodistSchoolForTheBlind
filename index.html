Welcome

A place where ability shines brighter than disability

Established in May 1958 by the Methodist Church, Ghana, we are a special school dedicated to the education of blind and low-vision learners, and for many years the only institution of its kind serving Ghana's three northern regions.

From kindergarten through junior high school and beyond, our learners follow the national curriculum in braille and large print, gain essential life skills, and prepare for further education or vocational careers. We believe every child deserves the chance to discover their potential, build independence and achieve their dreams.

  • ✓Inclusive, quality education following the Ghana Education Service syllabus.
  • ✓Holistic support: rehabilitation, orientation, mobility and daily-living skills.
  • ✓A supportive community where learners grow in confidence and character.

What We Offer

Learning pathways for every stage

Our programmes combine academic learning, rehabilitation and practical skills so that every learner can progress with confidence.

Basic School

Kindergarten to Junior High

Learners follow the national Ghana Education Service syllabus in braille and large print, preparing for secondary education and beyond.

Rehabilitation

Braille & Rehabilitation

Student Life

Confidence, creativity and community

Learning here goes far beyond the classroom. Our students sing, create, compete and lead, discovering talents and building the confidence to shape their own futures.

From our cultural troupe and music to inter-school competitions where our learners have won medals, student life at Wa Methodist is vibrant, joyful and full of possibility. Talented performers have even captured national attention, sharing their gifts with audiences across Ghana.
