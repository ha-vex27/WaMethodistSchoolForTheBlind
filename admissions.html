Admissions

We welcome blind and low-vision learners who are ready to learn, grow and thrive in a supportive community.

Who We Serve

A school built for blind & low-vision learners

Wa Methodist School for the Blind admits children and young people with visual impairment who need specialised, accessible education.

Our programmes cater to learners from kindergarten age upwards, including those who are new to blindness and need rehabilitation. Because we are residential, we also welcome learners from communities far from Wa who need a safe place to live and learn.

  • ✓Kindergarten to Junior High for academic learners.
  • ✓Rehabilitation for learners who become blind later in life.
  • ✓Vocational pathways for older learners seeking practical skills.

How to Apply

Four simple steps

1

Get in touch

Contact the school by phone or through our contact form to express interest and ask any questions.

2

Visit & assess

Arrange a visit so we can understand your child's needs and recommend the right programme.

3

Complete enrolment

Provide the required documents and complete registration with our admissions team.

4

Start learning

Join our community and begin a journey of learning, independence and friendship.

Requirements can change from year to year. Please contact the school for the current list of documents and enrolment dates.

Admissions FAQ

Yes. Wa Methodist School for the Blind is a residential school, providing accommodation for learners who travel from distant communities, especially important for safe, consistent attendance.
Academic learners follow the national Ghana Education Service syllabus in braille and large print from kindergarten to junior high school. Others join our rehabilitation or vocational programmes.
The school relies on government support, the Methodist Church and generous donors. If cost is a concern, please reach out: many learners receive assistance, and partners regularly help with fees, food and materials.
